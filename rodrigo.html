  • OLIVIA RODRIGO GUTS TOUR 2024 POSTER

    December 2023

    In my first course in graphic design, I designed a poster for Olivia Rodrigo’s 2024 GUTS World Tour. I wanted to emphasize the distressed and scrapbook aesthetics that a lot of her album and tour designs incorporate. To do that, I used a photo of Olivia Rodrigo and posterized it to give a grainy and saturated look. I wanted to emphasize a grunge rocker look which is a key component of several songs in her sophomore album GUTS. I drew heavy inspiration from 90s and 2000s aesthetics because these aesthetics are a part of her brand. I made a play on the phrase that Olivia Rodrigo mentions in relation to her newly released album GUTS, where she says she “spills her guts.” I designed this poster in Adobe Photoshop, sometimes also using Adobe Illustrator.
