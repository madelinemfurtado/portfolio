    ELEVATIONS: THE ART OF ANIMAL MIGRATION

    January - March 2025

    In Winter 2025, I took a course on Exhibition Design at UC Davis with the UC Davis Professor of Design Tim McNeil, who is a practicing exhibition designer. The thematic theme around our project was "international travel." When considering international travel, I immediately though about animal migratory patterns. I presented my idea to my team and we went through with my idea, creating an entire exhibition centered around animal migration and how it is impacted by human interventions. The process in creating the exhibition included selecting artworks, developing an exhibition narrative, dividing up the spatial layout, finding the graphic identity of the exhibition, and developing marketing materials for the exhibition.

    I collaborated with 2 UC Davis Design students for this project, Manreet Randhawa and Jorja Lindsey. Our group is featured on "Storied Spaces," a website dedicated towards projects undertaken at UC Davis surrounding experiential design.

    For this project, I contributed greatly towards every aspect of the curatorial development and designing of the exhibition. My main contributions to this assignment include our exhibition promotional signage, the title wall graphic design, the blueprint for the exhibition plan in Vectorworks, exhibition lighting blueprint, the 3D renderings of exhibition furniture in Rhino3D, and our curatorial premise.
