About Me

Hi! My name is Madeline Furtado. I am a designer and art historian based in California.

Raised in Stockton, California, my experiences growing up in a diverse community continuously inspire my designs and artistic voice.

I am specifically interested in creating experiences and programs that build community and foster an appreciation for the arts in local communities. My love for the arts stems from my belief in the arts to be a universal language that transcends past delineated boundaries.
