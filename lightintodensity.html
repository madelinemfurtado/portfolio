    LIGHT INTO DENSITY: ABSTRACT ENCOUNTERS 1920S-1960S EXHIBITION AT THE MANETTI SHREM MUSEUM OF ART

    July - September 2024

    I was a Guest Curator and Exhibition Designer for the Manetti Shrem Museum of Art, where I worked in curatorial and design teams to create an exhibition that focused on encouraging visitors to make their own interpretations of the artworks on display. During my time in this position, I drafted exhibition text and drafted mockups of the interior spaces, designed title wall mockups, and assisted with spatial planning through digital and physical renderings of the exhibition space. Our main goal: to create a visitor centered experience within the gallery space that encouraged visitor interpretation and engagement with modern art without worrying about their interpretations being considered "correct." The exhibition was on view at the Manetti Shrem Museum from September 2024 to May 2025.

    When designing the exhibition, I worked on this project in a team of 6 other UC Davis Design students, with UC Davis Design Professors Tim McNeil and Brett Snyder as our supervisors. You can find more information on this project through the website "Storied Spaces," a website dedicated towards projects undertaken at UC Davis surrounding experiential design. Light into Density: Abstract Encounters 1920s-1960s was featured in an article on the UC Davis Letters & Science Magazine, "Inside the Planning of an Art Exhibition," where I was interviewed to discuss my experience, the curation and design process, and the challenges involved in creating the exhibition.
